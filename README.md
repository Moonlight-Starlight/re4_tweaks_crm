Adds Camera Relative Movement options to Xinput controllers so you can move in the direction the left stick points, relative to the camera, instead of tank controls, has two options:

Chase camera: The camera follows the character, as in the original. Lean the stick to steer, push down to walk backwards.
Detached camera: The right stick orbits the camera, which stays put when the character turns. The left stick moves the character in every direction. (Currently unsmoothed)
